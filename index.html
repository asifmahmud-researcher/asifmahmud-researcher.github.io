PhD candidate in water treatment · IC2MP, Université de Poitiers

Asif Mahmud

I study the contaminants hiding in our water, now focusing on rubber additives and the halogenated by-products they form in drinking water. Trained as a soil scientist in Bangladesh, I completed the Erasmus Mundus master's in Environmental Contamination and Toxicology (ECT+) across France, Norway and Spain.

See my publicationsGoogle ScholarResearchGateLinkedIn

Research focus

My work combines analytical chemistry, molecular methods and spatial analysis to understand how contaminants move through water, soil and sediment, and what that means for ecosystems and public health.
